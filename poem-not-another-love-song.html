(not) another love song

I don't want another love song give me something else. I don't want a song about a boy I want to write about myself. I don't want another love song these boys are getting boring. I don't want a breakup tragedy I don't want another lover's story. I want a story about something more complex than just a boy who becomes subject to my attention and my desire because within me there's a fire of thoughts and ideas about problems that need to be solved so I search for the panacea... Around a boy my life does not revolve. It's about looking for answers to life's deepest questions, It's about my family and my everyday life. It's not about a man did I mention… Love just seems to waste my time.
