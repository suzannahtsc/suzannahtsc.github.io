Onward You Go!

Congratulations on your achievement! You’ve just made one more big step on your journey of life – But it’s not over just yet! Before you move on, I hope you’ll take a minute to reflect on all that you’ve done, and all that you should not forget. You’ve had ups and downs, but you have learned from them all. The rainy days were tough, but they’ve made you grow big and tall. And you are growing up so quick! But you are sure to keep growing… As long as you don’t quit, there is hope to keep hoping. After all, this is not the end – things are just getting started! So it’s okay to be a bit scared as you now enter waters uncharted… But as this new part of your life begins, as you start writing your next chapter— approach with confidence, be unafraid of joy and laughter. Because this part of your journey is not always going to be a breeze… It’s okay to stray from the road at times, when you are following your dreams. Remember those you love, and who love you back unconditionally. Although they may be far, keep them close to your heart, and you are sure to succeed. We are all so proud of you, and how far you have come. We can’t wait to see what you will do, and the person you will become.
