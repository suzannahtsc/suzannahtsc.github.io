Sister

I remember when you were a kid and all that we did In the summer and in the spring all the times that we would sing together in the car pretending we were Stars I always miss you in the winter and in the fall even though I'd never call You are my younger sister and whenever they'd ask I'd say "Of course I miss her!" But now it's time for you to leave to go and find your way it's your presence this time that we will grieve even though we know you could never stay. I'm so happy I got the chance to watch you grow up to be your partner in this dance I'm gonna miss you oh so much.
