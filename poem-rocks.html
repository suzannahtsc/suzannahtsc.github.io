Rocks

all these people without any depth they don't know how to talk about one thing or the next Is it too much to ask? for someone who doesn't mask all their insecurities and fears? Who doesn't wipe away all their tears? I just want to talk to someone who's not a rock. Maybe I'll find them in a foreign land, A place where the culture I don't understand, A place where life is lovely and grand, A place where the people are never too bland.
